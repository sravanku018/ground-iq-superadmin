Doctrine: Light Mode, Combined

[G] ONE GREY RAMP bg → surface-alt → surface-hover → surface on the way up; ink → secondary → muted → faint on the way down. Borders are always the ramp, never new greys — #e2e8f0 in the field, #dadce0 only inside the portal skin.
[G] FILL + TINT, ALWAYS A PAIR Every semantic color ships with its background tint — party colors, ok/warn/danger, accents. Fills mark data; tints mark surfaces. A tint without its fill is decoration; a fill without its tint has no selected state.
[A] POLITICAL COLOR MEANS A PARTY Congress green, BJP deep saffron #ea580c, BRS deep pink #db2777, Others indigo #6366f1 — used only where the number is a party. Everything else stays neutral. TDP yellow stays dropped: its lightness can't pass on white.
[A] DARK IS AN INSET, NOT A THEME Light mode is the only theme. Dark slate gradients (#0f172a → #1e293b) are reserved for focus moments — the update nudge, the field hero — with a sky border and a single glowing accent so they feel deliberate, not broken.
