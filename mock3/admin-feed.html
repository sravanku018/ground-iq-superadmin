Twitter Principles Applied

OPTIMISTIC Card appears instantly on submit. "Syncing…" dashed border shows network state without blocking the feed. Tap → instant, network catches up.
SCANNABLE One consistent card: avatar → name → location → answer pills → score → status. Eye scans left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Firehose made glanceable.
EXPLAINABLE Every score has a formula panel. "Why am I seeing this?" is always answered. No opaque algorithm — the score is derived, not guessed.
FEED MECHANICS Pull-to-refresh with spinner. Auto-refresh every 30s. Status filter chips. Live dot pulses. Cards stack newest-first. One job: show incoming data.
REAL-TIME New cards slide in with animation after 5s. Expandable detail on tap (GPS, photo, voice, answers). Filter by Pending/Confirmed/Rejected.

Caution from Twitter

  • No opaque ranking: feed is chronological, not algorithmic. Admin decides what matters.

  • Score stays explainable: the formula panel is always visible. The score is a tool, not a black box.
