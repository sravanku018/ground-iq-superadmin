Apple Principles Applied

SUBTRACT 48px min tap targets. One question per card at a time. No back button — swipe is the gesture. "Deciding what not to do."
CRAFT FEEL Elevation on cards (shadow-md), scale-down on press (0.96), progress ring with animated stroke-dashoffset, haptic ripple on tap. Motion = perceived speed.
DEFERENCE UI recedes — white cards, muted chrome. Content (question text, options) dominates. "Get out of the content's way."
DEPTH Single card fills the viewport. Animated card entrance (translateY + scale). Swipe gesture with 80px threshold. Progress strip = persistent context.
OFFLINE Online/offline chip in status bar. GPS + Photo + Voice lock indicators. "Built for the low end" — works on cheap phone, bad network.

Implementation Notes

  • Party-colored buttons: Congress green, BJP saffron, BRS pink — validated palette from tokens.css
  • Sentiment buttons: green/amber/red — instant visual categorization, zero cognitive load
  • Swipe cards: native touch events; 80px threshold; card entrance animation on transition
  • Offline-first: answers stored in IndexedDB; sync queue fires on reconnect; online/offline chip
  • Haptics: navigator.vibrate(10) + CSS ripple on selection — the "physicality of a swipe"
  • Lock strip: GPS ✓, Photo ✓, Voice — progressive disclosure of capture state
