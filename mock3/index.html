Design rationale:
  • Treatment A preserves party identity — critical for political data consumers who expect Congress=green, BJP=orange.
  • Treatment B trades identity for magnitude clarity — a single blue ramp makes relative counts immediately scannable, with party pill as identity anchor.
  • Both treatments rely on the "relief rule": every bar is direct-labeled, so color alone is never the only encoding.
  • TDP yellow dropped — inherent lightness fails contrast on white surface without forced darkening.
