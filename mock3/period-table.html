Doctrine: Data Legible as a System

[G] DATA LEGIBLE Google's genius: making enormous complex data legible. Same table component for Party, Districts, and Surveyors. Fixed columns, consistent alignment, tabular-nums for numbers. The eye learns one pattern.
[G] NOT TASTE-DRIVEN The "41 shades of blue" principle. Colors are validated (Congress=green, BJP=saffron, BRS=pink). Bar widths are computed from data, not eyeballed. The table is measured, not designed.
[G] PERIOD SWITCH Total / Today / Week / Month — same table, different slice. The period selector is a system-level control. Data stays legible across every time window.
